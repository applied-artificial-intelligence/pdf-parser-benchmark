Invoice result and continuation row

The retained comparison uses generated PDFs from six source template/complexity combinations, a fixed extractor and frozen task rules. Prior qualification included coding-agent visual review, not independent human adjudication or real-vendor sampling. The ten-item invoice used as an illustration was chosen to explain a continuation-row failure; it is not a representative estimate by itself.

Both configurations returned content on 100 inputs. Complete-task passes were 84 and 55, respectively. Record F1 is 2 × matched / (expected + predicted), giving 95.84% from 668 / 696 / 698 and 97.62% from 677 / 696 / 691.

In invoice 001, the continuation row has quantity 84.07 and amount $4,407,902.75. The pdfplumber raw output contains it after the nine-record Markdown table, in the plain-text section. The selected markdown_tables extraction path returned nine records and omitted that item. The pypdf combination returned ten. Both recovered all five requested fields on this document. These observations diagnose an extractor/representation interaction without implying that the raw text lacked the row.
