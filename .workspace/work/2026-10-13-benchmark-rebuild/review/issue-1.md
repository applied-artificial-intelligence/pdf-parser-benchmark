# Issue 1: Assess ParseBench coverage against meaningful business workloads

Requirement: Inventory all five annotation groups at pinned code/data revisions and reconcile page, document and case counts with launch/current primary sources. Preserve source URLs, revision IDs, file hashes and original domain/type labels. Inspect actual source pages and rules across declared domains, document forms and dimensions. Build a traceable workload-fit matrix covering reports, contracts/policies, transactional records/forms, presentations and technical documents, including language, scan/digital form, multipage context and downstream information needs. These are practical workload categories, not a representative survey of business. Unknown labels or uninspected documents stay unknown, not absent. Separate source availability, annotation presence and whether the metric tests the needed behavior.
