# Issue 2: Reconcile retained runs into one authoritative registry

Requirement: Returned reviews and dispositions authorize this issue. Extend the existing inventory and replace the story generator's independently maintained SLATE with registry-backed selection. Reconcile result/config links, checkpoints and merges, input/reference availability, historical selection, backend and wrapper identity, cache/timing unknowns and metric coverage. Keep all families and claimed historical values. Freeze the inventory input boundary before generating new exports. Preserve original counters/labels beside independently derived counts and claim dispositions. Record conflicting descriptions without asserting the entire old run is false. Do not reconstruct a past version from today's config. Establish an artifact classification and claim-specific tier with reasons and evidence paths.
