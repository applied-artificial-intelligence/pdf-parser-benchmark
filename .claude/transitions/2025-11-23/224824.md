**Ground truth mismatch discovered** - explains low invoice scores (30-44%):
- Ground truth generated from DOCX (`| table | format |`)
- PDF parsers extract raw text (no markdown)
- Different reading order, extra metadata in ground truth
- **Not a bug** - methodology difference

**Options to fix:**
1. Regenerate invoice ground truth from PDF text
2. Use TEDS metric instead of edit_similarity
3. Document as known limitation
