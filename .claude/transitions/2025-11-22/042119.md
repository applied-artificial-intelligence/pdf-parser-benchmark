## Current State

### Critical Finding: CUAD Ground Truth is Flawed
Discovered that CUAD dataset ground truth is **structurally flattened** - unsuitable for structure metrics (Element F1):
- Average ~1,458 chars/line (entire pages merged into single lines)
- Section markers (1.1, 1.2, etc.) concatenated without line breaks
- Structure was lost during original CUAD dataset creation (designed for clause extraction, not structure)

**Implication**: Element F1 rankings are INVERTED for CUAD:
- Parsers producing better structure score WORSE
- Parsers producing wall-of-text score HIGHER (match flattened GT)

- **Paper**: "CUAD: An Expert-Annotated NLP Dataset for Legal Contract Review" (arXiv:2103.06268)
- **Authors**: Dan Hendrycks, Collin Burns, Anya Chen, Spencer Ball
- **Organization**: The Atticus Project, Inc.
- **Purpose**: Clause extraction (Q&A), NOT document structure recovery
- **GitHub**: https://github.com/TheAtticusProject/cuad
