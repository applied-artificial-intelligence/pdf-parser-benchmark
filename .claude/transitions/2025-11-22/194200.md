## Unresolved Memory Issues

### The Big Problem
Even with "minimal" parsers (pymupdf, pdfplumber, pypdf, pypdfium2, pdfminer) and single worker, memory grew to **5GB** after ~3 minutes of processing.

### Observations
1. **Initial memory** after parser import: ~680MB (reasonable)
2. **Memory after 3 min**: 5GB and growing
3. **No checkpoint created** in 3 minutes (should checkpoint every 25 results)
4. CUAD documents are moderately large (up to 96 pages, 288KB ground truth)

### Likely Causes to Investigate
1. **Memory leak in runner**: Results or parsed content accumulating without release
2. **AST parser caching**: `MarkdownASTParser` in `runner.py` may be caching parsed ASTs
3. **Ground truth loading**: Each document's GT loaded repeatedly per parser
4. **Metric computation**: Tree similarity / element F1 may accumulate data

### Key Files to Investigate
- `pdf_bench/runner.py` - `_ast_parser` is module-level, may cache
- `pdf_bench/metrics/markdown_ast.py` - MarkdownASTParser implementation
- `pdf_bench/metrics/tree_edit_distance.py` - Tree similarity algorithm
