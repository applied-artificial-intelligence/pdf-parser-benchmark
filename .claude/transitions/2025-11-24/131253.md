## Summary

### Key Finding: The 7pp Gap is Misleading

The headline numbers (88.4% commercial vs 81.3% open-source) come from **different document sets**:
- Commercial: 200 documents (full coverage from Azure/AWS)
- Open-source: 353 documents (all corpora)

**On head-to-head comparison (180 common documents), open-source actually wins by 6.2 percentage points.**

### Head-to-Head Results (180 Documents)

| Domain | Docs | Azure Avg | Best OS Avg | Gap |
|--------|------|-----------|-------------|-----|
| HR/Resumes | 34 | 86.5% | 88.2% | -1.7% (OS wins) |
| Invoices | 100 | 43.9% | 50.9% | -7.0% (OS wins) |
| Legal Templates | 15 | 89.0% | 98.9% | -9.9% (OS wins) |
| Synthetic | 23 | 82.9% | 90.6% | -7.7% (OS wins) |
| **OVERALL** | **180** | **62.6%** | **68.8%** | **-6.2%** |

### Why the Discrepancy?

1. **Invoice penalty**: Azure scores 43.9% on invoices (100 docs = 56% of sample), dragging down overall average
2. **Document mix matters**: The published 88.4% excluded low-scoring invoice subset or used format-agnostic scores
3. **Legal templates show biggest gap**: Open-source hits 98.9% vs Azure's 89.0%

### Biggest Qualitative Differences

**Where Open-Source Excels**:
- Multi-column layouts: PyMuPDF 93% vs PdfPlumber 56% (31pp spread)
- Table structure: Invoice_072 shows 35pp gap (PyPDFium2 wins)
- Complex headers: 22pp average advantage

**Where Commercial Excels**:
- OCR (scanned documents): Marker 72.8% is only competitive open-source option
- API reliability: Azure/AWS 100% coverage vs Databricks 68%
- Dense flowing text: Minor advantage (~2-5pp)
