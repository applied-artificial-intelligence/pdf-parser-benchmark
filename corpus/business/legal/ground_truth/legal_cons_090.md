Effective Date: March 20, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc.
("Client")
and

Jennifer Evans
("Consultant")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc. desires to engage Jennifer Evans to provide certain services;
WHEREAS, Jennifer Evans has expertise and is willing to provide such services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing the engagement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Services
1.0.1 Jennifer Evans agrees to provide Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc. with consulting services as described in one or more Statements of Work to be mutually agreed upon by the parties (the "Services"). Each Statement of Work shall specify the scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees for the Services to be performed.
1.0.2 Jennifer Evans shall deliver to Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc. the work product and deliverables described in the applicable Statement of Work ("Deliverables"). All Deliverables shall be delivered in the format and by the deadlines specified in the Statement of Work. Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc. shall have 3 business days to review and approve each Deliverable.
2. Compensation
2.0.1 Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc. shall pay Jennifer Evans fees as specified in the applicable Statement of Work. Unless otherwise specified, fees shall be calculated at an hourly rate of $405 per hour. Jennifer Evans shall submit monthly invoices detailing hours worked and expenses incurred, and Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc. shall pay such invoices within 45 days of receipt.
2.0.2 Jennifer Evans shall be reimbursed for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with performing the Services, provided that such expenses are pre-approved by Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc. in writing and are supported by appropriate documentation. Travel expenses shall be reimbursed at coach class rates for air travel and standard rates for lodging and meals.
3. Independent Contractor Relationship
3.0.1 Jennifer Evans is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or joint venturer of Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc.. Jennifer Evans shall have sole control over the manner and means of performing the Services, subject to the specifications in the Statement of Work. Jennifer Evans shall be responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits relating to Jennifer Evans's performance of Services. Jennifer Evans shall not have authority to bind Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc. or to make representations on behalf of Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc..
4. Intellectual Property
4.0.1 All Work Product created by Jennifer Evans in connection with the Services shall be the sole and exclusive property of Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc.. "Work Product" means all deliverables, documents, materials, inventions, discoveries, and other work product created or developed by Jennifer Evans in performing the Services. Jennifer Evans hereby assigns to Crawford, Pena and Sandoval Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein.
5. General Provisions
5.1 Governing Law
5.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Massachusetts, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
5.2 Entire Agreement
5.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
