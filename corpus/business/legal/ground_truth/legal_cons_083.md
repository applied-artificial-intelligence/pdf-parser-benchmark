CONSULTING AGREEMENT

Effective Date: August 17, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Lopez Enterprises
("Client")
and

Boyer Inc LLC
("Consultant")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Lopez Enterprises desires to engage Boyer Inc LLC to provide certain services;
WHEREAS, Boyer Inc LLC has expertise and is willing to provide such services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing the engagement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Services
1.0.1 Boyer Inc LLC agrees to provide Lopez Enterprises with consulting services as described in one or more Statements of Work to be mutually agreed upon by the parties (the "Services"). Each Statement of Work shall specify the scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees for the Services to be performed.
1.0.2 Boyer Inc LLC shall deliver to Lopez Enterprises the work product and deliverables described in the applicable Statement of Work ("Deliverables"). All Deliverables shall be delivered in the format and by the deadlines specified in the Statement of Work. Lopez Enterprises shall have 5 business days to review and approve each Deliverable.
2. Compensation
2.0.1 Lopez Enterprises shall pay Boyer Inc LLC fees as specified in the applicable Statement of Work. Unless otherwise specified, fees shall be calculated at an hourly rate of $505 per hour. Boyer Inc LLC shall submit monthly invoices detailing hours worked and expenses incurred, and Lopez Enterprises shall pay such invoices within 30 days of receipt.
2.0.2 Boyer Inc LLC shall be reimbursed for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with performing the Services, provided that such expenses are pre-approved by Lopez Enterprises in writing and are supported by appropriate documentation. Travel expenses shall be reimbursed at coach class rates for air travel and standard rates for lodging and meals.
3. Independent Contractor Relationship
3.0.1 Boyer Inc LLC is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or joint venturer of Lopez Enterprises. Boyer Inc LLC shall have sole control over the manner and means of performing the Services, subject to the specifications in the Statement of Work. Boyer Inc LLC shall be responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits relating to Boyer Inc LLC's performance of Services. Boyer Inc LLC shall not have authority to bind Lopez Enterprises or to make representations on behalf of Lopez Enterprises.
4. Intellectual Property
4.0.1 All Work Product created by Boyer Inc LLC in connection with the Services shall be the sole and exclusive property of Lopez Enterprises. "Work Product" means all deliverables, documents, materials, inventions, discoveries, and other work product created or developed by Boyer Inc LLC in performing the Services. Boyer Inc LLC hereby assigns to Lopez Enterprises all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein.
5. General Provisions
5.1 Governing Law
5.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
5.2 Entire Agreement
5.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
