EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: August 16, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Cruz Ltd Inc.
("Company")
and

Pamela Holmes
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Cruz Ltd Inc. desires to employ Pamela Holmes in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Pamela Holmes wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Cruz Ltd Inc. hereby employs Pamela Holmes in the position of Software Engineer. Pamela Holmes shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Cruz Ltd Inc. from time to time. Pamela Holmes shall report to Director.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Cruz Ltd Inc. shall pay Pamela Holmes an annual base salary of $95,000, payable in accordance with Cruz Ltd Inc.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Pamela Holmes acknowledges that during employment, Pamela Holmes will have access to Confidential Information of Cruz Ltd Inc.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Cruz Ltd Inc.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Pamela Holmes agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Cruz Ltd Inc..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Pamela Holmes hereby assigns to Cruz Ltd Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Pamela Holmes, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Cruz Ltd Inc.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Cruz Ltd Inc.; or (c) are created using Cruz Ltd Inc.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Pamela Holmes agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Cruz Ltd Inc.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Massachusetts, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
