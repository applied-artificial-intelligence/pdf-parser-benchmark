CONSULTING AGREEMENT

Effective Date: May 25, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Hill-Diaz LLC
("Client")
and

Peters Ltd Ltd.
("Consultant")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Hill-Diaz LLC desires to engage Peters Ltd Ltd. to provide certain services;
WHEREAS, Peters Ltd Ltd. has expertise and is willing to provide such services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing the engagement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Services
1.0.1 Peters Ltd Ltd. agrees to provide Hill-Diaz LLC with consulting services as described in one or more Statements of Work to be mutually agreed upon by the parties (the "Services"). Each Statement of Work shall specify the scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees for the Services to be performed.
1.0.2 Peters Ltd Ltd. shall deliver to Hill-Diaz LLC the work product and deliverables described in the applicable Statement of Work ("Deliverables"). All Deliverables shall be delivered in the format and by the deadlines specified in the Statement of Work. Hill-Diaz LLC shall have 5 business days to review and approve each Deliverable.
2. Compensation
2.0.1 Hill-Diaz LLC shall pay Peters Ltd Ltd. fees as specified in the applicable Statement of Work. Unless otherwise specified, fees shall be calculated at an hourly rate of $235 per hour. Peters Ltd Ltd. shall submit monthly invoices detailing hours worked and expenses incurred, and Hill-Diaz LLC shall pay such invoices within 30 days of receipt.
2.0.2 Peters Ltd Ltd. shall be reimbursed for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with performing the Services, provided that such expenses are pre-approved by Hill-Diaz LLC in writing and are supported by appropriate documentation. Travel expenses shall be reimbursed at coach class rates for air travel and standard rates for lodging and meals.
3. Independent Contractor Relationship
3.0.1 Peters Ltd Ltd. is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or joint venturer of Hill-Diaz LLC. Peters Ltd Ltd. shall have sole control over the manner and means of performing the Services, subject to the specifications in the Statement of Work. Peters Ltd Ltd. shall be responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits relating to Peters Ltd Ltd.'s performance of Services. Peters Ltd Ltd. shall not have authority to bind Hill-Diaz LLC or to make representations on behalf of Hill-Diaz LLC.
4. Intellectual Property
4.0.1 All Work Product created by Peters Ltd Ltd. in connection with the Services shall be the sole and exclusive property of Hill-Diaz LLC. "Work Product" means all deliverables, documents, materials, inventions, discoveries, and other work product created or developed by Peters Ltd Ltd. in performing the Services. Peters Ltd Ltd. hereby assigns to Hill-Diaz LLC all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein.
5. General Provisions
5.1 Governing Law
5.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
5.2 Entire Agreement
5.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
