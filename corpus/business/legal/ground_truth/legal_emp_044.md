EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: July 20, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Jordan Enterprises
("Company")
and

Jeremy Ramirez
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Jordan Enterprises desires to employ Jeremy Ramirez in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Jeremy Ramirez wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Jordan Enterprises hereby employs Jeremy Ramirez in the position of VP of Engineering. Jeremy Ramirez shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Jordan Enterprises from time to time. Jeremy Ramirez shall report to President.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Jordan Enterprises shall pay Jeremy Ramirez an annual base salary of $155,000, payable in accordance with Jordan Enterprises's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Jeremy Ramirez acknowledges that during employment, Jeremy Ramirez will have access to Confidential Information of Jordan Enterprises. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Jordan Enterprises's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Jeremy Ramirez agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Jordan Enterprises.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Jeremy Ramirez hereby assigns to Jordan Enterprises all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Jeremy Ramirez, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Jordan Enterprises's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Jordan Enterprises; or (c) are created using Jordan Enterprises's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Jeremy Ramirez agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Jordan Enterprises's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
