EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: August 14, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc.
("Company")
and

Mark Smith
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc. desires to employ Mark Smith in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Mark Smith wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc. hereby employs Mark Smith in the position of Chief Marketing Officer. Mark Smith shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc. from time to time. Mark Smith shall report to President.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc. shall pay Mark Smith an annual base salary of $230,000, payable in accordance with Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Mark Smith acknowledges that during employment, Mark Smith will have access to Confidential Information of Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Mark Smith agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Mark Smith hereby assigns to Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Mark Smith, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc.; or (c) are created using Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Mark Smith agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Stevens, Bell and Cortez Inc.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
