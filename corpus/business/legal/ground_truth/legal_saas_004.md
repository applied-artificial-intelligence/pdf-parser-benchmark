RECITALS

WHEREAS, Hess, Harper and King LLC offers a cloud-based software platform;
WHEREAS, Simon-Williams Corporation desires to subscribe to and use the Services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing such use;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions
1.0.1 Hess, Harper and King LLC shall provide Simon-Williams Corporation with access to the Enterprise Portal platform (the "Service"), a cloud-based software solution designed to automate workflow and business processes. The Service shall be made available via the Internet in accordance with the service level agreement set forth in this Agreement.
2. Services
2.1 Service Level Agreement
2.1.1 Hess, Harper and King LLC shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Service available 99.95% of the time, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance and downtime caused by factors beyond Hess, Harper and King LLC's reasonable control. Scheduled maintenance shall be performed during off-peak hours with at least 72 hours' advance notice to Simon-Williams Corporation.
2.2 Support Services
2.2.1 Hess, Harper and King LLC shall provide Simon-Williams Corporation with technical support services via email during normal business hours (8am-6pm PST). Response times shall be: (a) 1 hours for critical issues affecting core functionality; (b) 12 hours for high-priority issues; and (c) 72 hours for normal priority requests.
3. Subscription and Fees
3.1 Subscription Plans
3.1.1 Simon-Williams Corporation shall subscribe to the Premium plan at a monthly subscription fee of $14166. The subscription includes 5 user accounts and 50 GB of data storage. Additional users may be added at $30 per user per month.
3.2 Payment Terms
3.2.1 All fees are due and payable in advance on a monthly basis. Hess, Harper and King LLC shall invoice Simon-Williams Corporation on or about the first day of each month, and payment shall be due within 30 days of the invoice date. Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 2.0% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.
4. Data and Privacy
4.1 Data Ownership
4.1.1 Simon-Williams Corporation retains all right, title, and interest in and to all data, content, and materials provided by Simon-Williams Corporation or its users to the Service ("Customer Data"). Hess, Harper and King LLC shall not access, use, or disclose Customer Data except as necessary to provide the Service or as required by law.
4.2 Data Security
4.2.1 Hess, Harper and King LLC shall implement and maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Such safeguards shall include, at a minimum: (a) data encryption in transit and at rest; (b) regular security assessments and penetration testing; (c) employee background checks and security training; and (d) incident response procedures.
5. Term and Termination
5.1 Initial Term
5.1.1 This Agreement shall commence on January 02, 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue for an initial term of 36 months (the "Initial Term"). Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 12-month periods (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days prior to the end of the then-current term.
6. Limitation of Liability
6.0.1 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUE, OR LOST DATA, EVEN IF SUCH PARTY HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES. EXCEPT FOR LIABILITY ARISING FROM GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE TO SERVICE PROVIDER IN THE 6 MONTHS PRECEDING THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY.

7.1 Governing Law
7.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
7.2 Entire Agreement
7.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
7.3 Notices
7.3.1 All notices, requests, demands, and other communications under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed to have been duly given: (a) on the date of delivery if delivered personally; (b) on the first business day following the date of dispatch if delivered by a nationally recognized next-day courier service; or (c) on the third business day following the date of mailing if delivered by registered or certified mail, return receipt requested, postage prepaid.
