RECITALS

WHEREAS, Humphrey, Gordon and Santiago Ltd. offers a cloud-based software platform;
WHEREAS, Simmons-Richmond Inc. desires to subscribe to and use the Services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing such use;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions
1.0.1 Humphrey, Gordon and Santiago Ltd. shall provide Simmons-Richmond Inc. with access to the Cloud Portal platform (the "Service"), a cloud-based software solution designed to facilitate project management and team collaboration. The Service shall be made available via the Internet in accordance with the service level agreement set forth in this Agreement.
2. Services
2.1 Service Level Agreement
2.1.1 Humphrey, Gordon and Santiago Ltd. shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Service available 99.9% of the time, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance and downtime caused by factors beyond Humphrey, Gordon and Santiago Ltd.'s reasonable control. Scheduled maintenance shall be performed during off-peak hours with at least 48 hours' advance notice to Simmons-Richmond Inc..
2.2 Support Services
2.2.1 Humphrey, Gordon and Santiago Ltd. shall provide Simmons-Richmond Inc. with technical support services via email during normal business hours (8am-6pm PST). Response times shall be: (a) 4 hours for critical issues affecting core functionality; (b) 8 hours for high-priority issues; and (c) 24 hours for normal priority requests.
3. Subscription and Fees
3.1 Subscription Plans
3.1.1 Simmons-Richmond Inc. shall subscribe to the Professional plan at a monthly subscription fee of $34166. The subscription includes 100 user accounts and 500 GB of data storage. Additional users may be added at $10 per user per month.
3.2 Payment Terms
3.2.1 All fees are due and payable in advance on a monthly basis. Humphrey, Gordon and Santiago Ltd. shall invoice Simmons-Richmond Inc. on or about the first day of each month, and payment shall be due within 45 days of the invoice date. Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.
4. Data and Privacy
4.1 Data Ownership
4.1.1 Simmons-Richmond Inc. retains all right, title, and interest in and to all data, content, and materials provided by Simmons-Richmond Inc. or its users to the Service ("Customer Data"). Humphrey, Gordon and Santiago Ltd. shall not access, use, or disclose Customer Data except as necessary to provide the Service or as required by law.
4.2 Data Security
4.2.1 Humphrey, Gordon and Santiago Ltd. shall implement and maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Such safeguards shall include, at a minimum: (a) data encryption in transit and at rest; (b) regular security assessments and penetration testing; (c) employee background checks and security training; and (d) incident response procedures.
5. Term and Termination
5.1 Initial Term
5.1.1 This Agreement shall commence on June 06, 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue for an initial term of 21 months (the "Initial Term"). Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 36-month periods (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days prior to the end of the then-current term.

6.0.1 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUE, OR LOST DATA, EVEN IF SUCH PARTY HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.
