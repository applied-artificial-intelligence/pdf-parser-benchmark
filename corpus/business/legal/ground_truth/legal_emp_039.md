EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: July 12, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Jones Enterprises
("Company")
and

Misty Nguyen
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Jones Enterprises desires to employ Misty Nguyen in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Misty Nguyen wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Jones Enterprises hereby employs Misty Nguyen in the position of VP of Operations. Misty Nguyen shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Jones Enterprises from time to time. Misty Nguyen shall report to CEO.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Jones Enterprises shall pay Misty Nguyen an annual base salary of $260,000, payable in accordance with Jones Enterprises's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Misty Nguyen acknowledges that during employment, Misty Nguyen will have access to Confidential Information of Jones Enterprises. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Jones Enterprises's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Misty Nguyen agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Jones Enterprises.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Misty Nguyen hereby assigns to Jones Enterprises all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Misty Nguyen, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Jones Enterprises's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Jones Enterprises; or (c) are created using Jones Enterprises's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Misty Nguyen agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Jones Enterprises's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
