EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: September 25, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Moss Enterprises
("Company")
and

Melissa Barrera
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Moss Enterprises desires to employ Melissa Barrera in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Melissa Barrera wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Moss Enterprises hereby employs Melissa Barrera in the position of Data Analyst. Melissa Barrera shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Moss Enterprises from time to time. Melissa Barrera shall report to Senior Manager.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Moss Enterprises shall pay Melissa Barrera an annual base salary of $105,000, payable in accordance with Moss Enterprises's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Melissa Barrera acknowledges that during employment, Melissa Barrera will have access to Confidential Information of Moss Enterprises. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Moss Enterprises's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Melissa Barrera agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Moss Enterprises.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Melissa Barrera hereby assigns to Moss Enterprises all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Melissa Barrera, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Moss Enterprises's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Moss Enterprises; or (c) are created using Moss Enterprises's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Melissa Barrera agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Moss Enterprises's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
