EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: October 13, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Rice-Maddox Corporation
("Company")
and

Jonathan White
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Rice-Maddox Corporation desires to employ Jonathan White in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Jonathan White wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Rice-Maddox Corporation hereby employs Jonathan White in the position of Chief Revenue Officer. Jonathan White shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Rice-Maddox Corporation from time to time. Jonathan White shall report to CEO.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Rice-Maddox Corporation shall pay Jonathan White an annual base salary of $155,000, payable in accordance with Rice-Maddox Corporation's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Jonathan White acknowledges that during employment, Jonathan White will have access to Confidential Information of Rice-Maddox Corporation. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Rice-Maddox Corporation's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Jonathan White agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Rice-Maddox Corporation.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Jonathan White hereby assigns to Rice-Maddox Corporation all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Jonathan White, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Rice-Maddox Corporation's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Rice-Maddox Corporation; or (c) are created using Rice-Maddox Corporation's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Jonathan White agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Rice-Maddox Corporation's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
