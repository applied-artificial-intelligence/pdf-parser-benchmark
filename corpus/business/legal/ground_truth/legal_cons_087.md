Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc.
("Client")

("Consultant")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc. desires to engage Nicolas Lewis to provide certain services;
WHEREAS, Nicolas Lewis has expertise and is willing to provide such services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing the engagement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Services
1.0.1 Nicolas Lewis agrees to provide Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc. with consulting services as described in one or more Statements of Work to be mutually agreed upon by the parties (the "Services"). Each Statement of Work shall specify the scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees for the Services to be performed.
1.0.2 Nicolas Lewis shall deliver to Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc. the work product and deliverables described in the applicable Statement of Work ("Deliverables"). All Deliverables shall be delivered in the format and by the deadlines specified in the Statement of Work. Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc. shall have 10 business days to review and approve each Deliverable.
2. Compensation
2.0.1 Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc. shall pay Nicolas Lewis fees as specified in the applicable Statement of Work. Unless otherwise specified, fees shall be calculated at an hourly rate of $175 per hour. Nicolas Lewis shall submit monthly invoices detailing hours worked and expenses incurred, and Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc. shall pay such invoices within 30 days of receipt.
2.0.2 Nicolas Lewis shall be reimbursed for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with performing the Services, provided that such expenses are pre-approved by Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc. in writing and are supported by appropriate documentation. Travel expenses shall be reimbursed at coach class rates for air travel and standard rates for lodging and meals.
3. Independent Contractor Relationship
3.0.1 Nicolas Lewis is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or joint venturer of Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc.. Nicolas Lewis shall have sole control over the manner and means of performing the Services, subject to the specifications in the Statement of Work. Nicolas Lewis shall be responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits relating to Nicolas Lewis's performance of Services. Nicolas Lewis shall not have authority to bind Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc. or to make representations on behalf of Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc..
4. Intellectual Property
4.0.1 All Work Product created by Nicolas Lewis in connection with the Services shall be the sole and exclusive property of Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc.. "Work Product" means all deliverables, documents, materials, inventions, discoveries, and other work product created or developed by Nicolas Lewis in performing the Services. Nicolas Lewis hereby assigns to Mcintyre, Baker and Burns Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein.
5. General Provisions
5.1 Governing Law
5.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
5.2 Entire Agreement
5.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
