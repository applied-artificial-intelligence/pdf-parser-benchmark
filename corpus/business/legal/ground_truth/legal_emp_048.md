EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: March 25, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc.
("Company")
and

Ronald Dawson
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc. desires to employ Ronald Dawson in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Ronald Dawson wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc. hereby employs Ronald Dawson in the position of VP of Operations. Ronald Dawson shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc. from time to time. Ronald Dawson shall report to CEO.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc. shall pay Ronald Dawson an annual base salary of $170,000, payable in accordance with Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Ronald Dawson acknowledges that during employment, Ronald Dawson will have access to Confidential Information of Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Ronald Dawson agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Ronald Dawson hereby assigns to Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Ronald Dawson, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc.; or (c) are created using Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Ronald Dawson agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Montgomery, Mccullough and Nelson Inc.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
