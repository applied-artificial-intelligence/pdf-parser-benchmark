CONSULTING AGREEMENT

Effective Date: May 23, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation
("Client")
and

Jeffrey Randall
("Consultant")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation desires to engage Jeffrey Randall to provide certain services;
WHEREAS, Jeffrey Randall has expertise and is willing to provide such services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing the engagement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Services
1.0.1 Jeffrey Randall agrees to provide Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation with consulting services as described in one or more Statements of Work to be mutually agreed upon by the parties (the "Services"). Each Statement of Work shall specify the scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees for the Services to be performed.
1.0.2 Jeffrey Randall shall deliver to Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation the work product and deliverables described in the applicable Statement of Work ("Deliverables"). All Deliverables shall be delivered in the format and by the deadlines specified in the Statement of Work. Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation shall have 7 business days to review and approve each Deliverable.
2. Compensation
2.0.1 Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation shall pay Jeffrey Randall fees as specified in the applicable Statement of Work. Unless otherwise specified, fees shall be calculated at an hourly rate of $420 per hour. Jeffrey Randall shall submit monthly invoices detailing hours worked and expenses incurred, and Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation shall pay such invoices within 15 days of receipt.
2.0.2 Jeffrey Randall shall be reimbursed for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with performing the Services, provided that such expenses are pre-approved by Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation in writing and are supported by appropriate documentation. Travel expenses shall be reimbursed at coach class rates for air travel and standard rates for lodging and meals.
3. Independent Contractor Relationship
3.0.1 Jeffrey Randall is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or joint venturer of Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation. Jeffrey Randall shall have sole control over the manner and means of performing the Services, subject to the specifications in the Statement of Work. Jeffrey Randall shall be responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits relating to Jeffrey Randall's performance of Services. Jeffrey Randall shall not have authority to bind Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation or to make representations on behalf of Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation.
4. Intellectual Property
4.0.1 All Work Product created by Jeffrey Randall in connection with the Services shall be the sole and exclusive property of Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation. "Work Product" means all deliverables, documents, materials, inventions, discoveries, and other work product created or developed by Jeffrey Randall in performing the Services. Jeffrey Randall hereby assigns to Reed, Jackson and Stevens Corporation all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein.
5. General Provisions
5.1 Governing Law
5.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
5.2 Entire Agreement
5.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
