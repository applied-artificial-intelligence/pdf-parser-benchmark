Effective Date: February 15, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Duke LLC LLC
("Company")
and

("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Duke LLC LLC desires to employ Jared Hernandez in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Jared Hernandez wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Duke LLC LLC hereby employs Jared Hernandez in the position of Data Analyst. Jared Hernandez shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Duke LLC LLC from time to time. Jared Hernandez shall report to Team Lead.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Duke LLC LLC shall pay Jared Hernandez an annual base salary of $175,000, payable in accordance with Duke LLC LLC's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Jared Hernandez acknowledges that during employment, Jared Hernandez will have access to Confidential Information of Duke LLC LLC. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Duke LLC LLC's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Jared Hernandez agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Duke LLC LLC.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Jared Hernandez hereby assigns to Duke LLC LLC all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Jared Hernandez, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Duke LLC LLC's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Duke LLC LLC; or (c) are created using Duke LLC LLC's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Jared Hernandez agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Duke LLC LLC's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
