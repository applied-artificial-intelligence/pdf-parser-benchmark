EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: February 02, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd.
("Company")
and

Margaret Todd
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd. desires to employ Margaret Todd in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Margaret Todd wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd. hereby employs Margaret Todd in the position of Data Analyst. Margaret Todd shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd. from time to time. Margaret Todd shall report to Director.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd. shall pay Margaret Todd an annual base salary of $270,000, payable in accordance with Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Margaret Todd acknowledges that during employment, Margaret Todd will have access to Confidential Information of Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Margaret Todd agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Margaret Todd hereby assigns to Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Margaret Todd, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd.; or (c) are created using Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Margaret Todd agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Lopez, Skinner and Castro Ltd.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
