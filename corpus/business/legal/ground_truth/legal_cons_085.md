CONSULTING AGREEMENT

Effective Date: August 27, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd.
("Client")
and

Miss Christine Mitchell
("Consultant")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd. desires to engage Miss Christine Mitchell to provide certain services;
WHEREAS, Miss Christine Mitchell has expertise and is willing to provide such services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing the engagement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Services
1.0.1 Miss Christine Mitchell agrees to provide Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd. with consulting services as described in one or more Statements of Work to be mutually agreed upon by the parties (the "Services"). Each Statement of Work shall specify the scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees for the Services to be performed.
1.0.2 Miss Christine Mitchell shall deliver to Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd. the work product and deliverables described in the applicable Statement of Work ("Deliverables"). All Deliverables shall be delivered in the format and by the deadlines specified in the Statement of Work. Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd. shall have 10 business days to review and approve each Deliverable.
2. Compensation
2.0.1 Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd. shall pay Miss Christine Mitchell fees as specified in the applicable Statement of Work. Unless otherwise specified, fees shall be calculated at an hourly rate of $135 per hour. Miss Christine Mitchell shall submit monthly invoices detailing hours worked and expenses incurred, and Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd. shall pay such invoices within 30 days of receipt.
2.0.2 Miss Christine Mitchell shall be reimbursed for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with performing the Services, provided that such expenses are pre-approved by Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd. in writing and are supported by appropriate documentation. Travel expenses shall be reimbursed at coach class rates for air travel and standard rates for lodging and meals.
3. Independent Contractor Relationship
3.0.1 Miss Christine Mitchell is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or joint venturer of Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd.. Miss Christine Mitchell shall have sole control over the manner and means of performing the Services, subject to the specifications in the Statement of Work. Miss Christine Mitchell shall be responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits relating to Miss Christine Mitchell's performance of Services. Miss Christine Mitchell shall not have authority to bind Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd. or to make representations on behalf of Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd..
4. Intellectual Property
4.0.1 All Work Product created by Miss Christine Mitchell in connection with the Services shall be the sole and exclusive property of Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd.. "Work Product" means all deliverables, documents, materials, inventions, discoveries, and other work product created or developed by Miss Christine Mitchell in performing the Services. Miss Christine Mitchell hereby assigns to Harris, Christensen and Hayes Ltd. all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein.
5. General Provisions
5.1 Governing Law
5.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
5.2 Entire Agreement
5.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
