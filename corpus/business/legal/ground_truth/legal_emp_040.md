EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: August 12, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd.
("Company")
and

Steven Richardson
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd. desires to employ Steven Richardson in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Steven Richardson wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd. hereby employs Steven Richardson in the position of Chief Technology Officer. Steven Richardson shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd. from time to time. Steven Richardson shall report to CEO.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd. shall pay Steven Richardson an annual base salary of $110,000, payable in accordance with Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Steven Richardson acknowledges that during employment, Steven Richardson will have access to Confidential Information of Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Steven Richardson agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Steven Richardson hereby assigns to Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Steven Richardson, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd.; or (c) are created using Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Steven Richardson agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Rice, Castaneda and Brown Ltd.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
