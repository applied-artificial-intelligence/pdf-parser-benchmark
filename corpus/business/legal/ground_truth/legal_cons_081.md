CONSULTING AGREEMENT

Effective Date: June 11, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Greene Solutions
("Client")
and

Stewart Systems
("Consultant")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Greene Solutions desires to engage Stewart Systems to provide certain services;
WHEREAS, Stewart Systems has expertise and is willing to provide such services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing the engagement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Services
1.0.1 Stewart Systems agrees to provide Greene Solutions with consulting services as described in one or more Statements of Work to be mutually agreed upon by the parties (the "Services"). Each Statement of Work shall specify the scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees for the Services to be performed.
1.0.2 Stewart Systems shall deliver to Greene Solutions the work product and deliverables described in the applicable Statement of Work ("Deliverables"). All Deliverables shall be delivered in the format and by the deadlines specified in the Statement of Work. Greene Solutions shall have 5 business days to review and approve each Deliverable.
2. Compensation
2.0.1 Greene Solutions shall pay Stewart Systems fees as specified in the applicable Statement of Work. Unless otherwise specified, fees shall be calculated at an hourly rate of $210 per hour. Stewart Systems shall submit monthly invoices detailing hours worked and expenses incurred, and Greene Solutions shall pay such invoices within 45 days of receipt.
2.0.2 Stewart Systems shall be reimbursed for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with performing the Services, provided that such expenses are pre-approved by Greene Solutions in writing and are supported by appropriate documentation. Travel expenses shall be reimbursed at coach class rates for air travel and standard rates for lodging and meals.
3. Independent Contractor Relationship
3.0.1 Stewart Systems is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or joint venturer of Greene Solutions. Stewart Systems shall have sole control over the manner and means of performing the Services, subject to the specifications in the Statement of Work. Stewart Systems shall be responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits relating to Stewart Systems's performance of Services. Stewart Systems shall not have authority to bind Greene Solutions or to make representations on behalf of Greene Solutions.
4. Intellectual Property
4.0.1 All Work Product created by Stewart Systems in connection with the Services shall be the sole and exclusive property of Greene Solutions. "Work Product" means all deliverables, documents, materials, inventions, discoveries, and other work product created or developed by Stewart Systems in performing the Services. Stewart Systems hereby assigns to Greene Solutions all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein.
5. General Provisions
5.1 Governing Law
5.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
5.2 Entire Agreement
5.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
