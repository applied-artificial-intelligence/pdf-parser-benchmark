EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: October 06, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Richardson PLC Inc.
("Company")
and

Steven Freeman
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Richardson PLC Inc. desires to employ Steven Freeman in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Steven Freeman wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Richardson PLC Inc. hereby employs Steven Freeman in the position of VP of Operations. Steven Freeman shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Richardson PLC Inc. from time to time. Steven Freeman shall report to C-Suite Executive.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Richardson PLC Inc. shall pay Steven Freeman an annual base salary of $255,000, payable in accordance with Richardson PLC Inc.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Steven Freeman acknowledges that during employment, Steven Freeman will have access to Confidential Information of Richardson PLC Inc.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Richardson PLC Inc.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Steven Freeman agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Richardson PLC Inc..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Steven Freeman hereby assigns to Richardson PLC Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Steven Freeman, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Richardson PLC Inc.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Richardson PLC Inc.; or (c) are created using Richardson PLC Inc.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Steven Freeman agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Richardson PLC Inc.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
