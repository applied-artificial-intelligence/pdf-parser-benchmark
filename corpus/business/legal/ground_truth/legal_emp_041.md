EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: December 25, 2024

BY AND BETWEEN

Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation
("Company")
and

Bradley Wells
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation desires to employ Bradley Wells in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Bradley Wells wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation hereby employs Bradley Wells in the position of Software Engineer. Bradley Wells shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation from time to time. Bradley Wells shall report to Senior Manager.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation shall pay Bradley Wells an annual base salary of $115,000, payable in accordance with Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Bradley Wells acknowledges that during employment, Bradley Wells will have access to Confidential Information of Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Bradley Wells agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Bradley Wells hereby assigns to Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Bradley Wells, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation; or (c) are created using Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Bradley Wells agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Mack, Jones and Atkinson Corporation's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
