EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: April 16, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Reynolds Solutions
("Company")
and

Michael Williams
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Reynolds Solutions desires to employ Michael Williams in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Michael Williams wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Reynolds Solutions hereby employs Michael Williams in the position of Director of Marketing. Michael Williams shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Reynolds Solutions from time to time. Michael Williams shall report to C-Suite Executive.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Reynolds Solutions shall pay Michael Williams an annual base salary of $150,000, payable in accordance with Reynolds Solutions's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Michael Williams acknowledges that during employment, Michael Williams will have access to Confidential Information of Reynolds Solutions. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Reynolds Solutions's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Michael Williams agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Reynolds Solutions.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Michael Williams hereby assigns to Reynolds Solutions all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Michael Williams, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Reynolds Solutions's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Reynolds Solutions; or (c) are created using Reynolds Solutions's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Michael Williams agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Reynolds Solutions's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
