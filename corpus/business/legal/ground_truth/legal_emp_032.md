EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: November 29, 2024

BY AND BETWEEN

Mitchell Enterprises
("Company")
and

Brian Miller
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Mitchell Enterprises desires to employ Brian Miller in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Brian Miller wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Mitchell Enterprises hereby employs Brian Miller in the position of VP of Engineering. Brian Miller shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Mitchell Enterprises from time to time. Brian Miller shall report to President.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Mitchell Enterprises shall pay Brian Miller an annual base salary of $98,000, payable in accordance with Mitchell Enterprises's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Brian Miller acknowledges that during employment, Brian Miller will have access to Confidential Information of Mitchell Enterprises. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Mitchell Enterprises's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Brian Miller agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Mitchell Enterprises.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Brian Miller hereby assigns to Mitchell Enterprises all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Brian Miller, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Mitchell Enterprises's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Mitchell Enterprises; or (c) are created using Mitchell Enterprises's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Brian Miller agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Mitchell Enterprises's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
