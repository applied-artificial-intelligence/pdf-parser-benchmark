EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: September 28, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Underwood LLC Inc.
("Company")
and

Stephanie Ross
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Underwood LLC Inc. desires to employ Stephanie Ross in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Stephanie Ross wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Underwood LLC Inc. hereby employs Stephanie Ross in the position of Sales Director. Stephanie Ross shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Underwood LLC Inc. from time to time. Stephanie Ross shall report to CEO.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Underwood LLC Inc. shall pay Stephanie Ross an annual base salary of $110,000, payable in accordance with Underwood LLC Inc.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Stephanie Ross acknowledges that during employment, Stephanie Ross will have access to Confidential Information of Underwood LLC Inc.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Underwood LLC Inc.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Stephanie Ross agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Underwood LLC Inc..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Stephanie Ross hereby assigns to Underwood LLC Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Stephanie Ross, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Underwood LLC Inc.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Underwood LLC Inc.; or (c) are created using Underwood LLC Inc.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Stephanie Ross agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Underwood LLC Inc.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
