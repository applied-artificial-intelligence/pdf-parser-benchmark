RECITALS

WHEREAS, Hobbs LLC Ltd. offers a cloud-based software platform;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions
1.0.1 Hobbs LLC Ltd. shall provide Ali Systems with access to the Enterprise Portal platform (the "Service"), a cloud-based software solution designed to automate workflow and business processes. The Service shall be made available via the Internet in accordance with the service level agreement set forth in this Agreement.
2. Services
2.1 Service Level Agreement
2.1.1 Hobbs LLC Ltd. shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Service available 99.5% of the time, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance and downtime caused by factors beyond Hobbs LLC Ltd.'s reasonable control. Scheduled maintenance shall be performed during off-peak hours with at least 48 hours' advance notice to Ali Systems.
2.2 Support Services
2.2.1 Hobbs LLC Ltd. shall provide Ali Systems with technical support services via email during normal business hours (Monday-Friday 9am-5pm). Response times shall be: (a) 2 hours for critical issues affecting core functionality; (b) 12 hours for high-priority issues; and (c) 72 hours for normal priority requests.
3. Subscription and Fees
3.1 Subscription Plans
3.1.1 Ali Systems shall subscribe to the Premium plan at a monthly subscription fee of $25833. The subscription includes 25 user accounts and 10 GB of data storage. Additional users may be added at $15 per user per month.
3.2 Payment Terms
3.2.1 All fees are due and payable in advance on a monthly basis. Hobbs LLC Ltd. shall invoice Ali Systems on or about the first day of each month, and payment shall be due within 45 days of the invoice date. Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.
4. Data and Privacy
4.1 Data Ownership
4.1.1 Ali Systems retains all right, title, and interest in and to all data, content, and materials provided by Ali Systems or its users to the Service ("Customer Data"). Hobbs LLC Ltd. shall not access, use, or disclose Customer Data except as necessary to provide the Service or as required by law.
4.2 Data Security
4.2.1 Hobbs LLC Ltd. shall implement and maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Such safeguards shall include, at a minimum: (a) data encryption in transit and at rest; (b) regular security assessments and penetration testing; (c) employee background checks and security training; and (d) incident response procedures.
5. Term and Termination
5.1 Initial Term
5.1.1 This Agreement shall commence on April 05, 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue for an initial term of 20 months (the "Initial Term"). Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 24-month periods (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days prior to the end of the then-current term.
