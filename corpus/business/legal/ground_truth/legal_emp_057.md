EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: August 20, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd.
("Company")
and

Michael Dudley
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd. desires to employ Michael Dudley in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Michael Dudley wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd. hereby employs Michael Dudley in the position of Product Manager. Michael Dudley shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd. from time to time. Michael Dudley shall report to Senior Director.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd. shall pay Michael Dudley an annual base salary of $90,000, payable in accordance with Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Michael Dudley acknowledges that during employment, Michael Dudley will have access to Confidential Information of Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Michael Dudley agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Michael Dudley hereby assigns to Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Michael Dudley, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd.; or (c) are created using Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Michael Dudley agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Pena, Harris and Macdonald Ltd.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Massachusetts, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
