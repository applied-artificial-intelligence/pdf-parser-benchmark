EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: August 29, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Brown-Vaughan Ltd.
("Company")
and

Kenneth Collins
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Brown-Vaughan Ltd. desires to employ Kenneth Collins in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Kenneth Collins wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Brown-Vaughan Ltd. hereby employs Kenneth Collins in the position of Sales Representative. Kenneth Collins shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Brown-Vaughan Ltd. from time to time. Kenneth Collins shall report to Team Lead.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Brown-Vaughan Ltd. shall pay Kenneth Collins an annual base salary of $220,000, payable in accordance with Brown-Vaughan Ltd.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Kenneth Collins acknowledges that during employment, Kenneth Collins will have access to Confidential Information of Brown-Vaughan Ltd.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Brown-Vaughan Ltd.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Kenneth Collins agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Brown-Vaughan Ltd..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Kenneth Collins hereby assigns to Brown-Vaughan Ltd. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Kenneth Collins, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Brown-Vaughan Ltd.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Brown-Vaughan Ltd.; or (c) are created using Brown-Vaughan Ltd.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Kenneth Collins agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Brown-Vaughan Ltd.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
