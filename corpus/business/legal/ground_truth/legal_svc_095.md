SERVICE AGREEMENT

Effective Date: November 14, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

King-Carter Corporation
("Service Provider")
and

Bailey-David Inc.
("Customer")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Bailey-David Inc. requires certain services to be provided;
WHEREAS, King-Carter Corporation is in the business of providing such services;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to enter into an agreement for the provision of services;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Services
1.0.1 King-Carter Corporation shall provide Bailey-David Inc. with facility maintenance and janitorial services (the "Services") in accordance with the Service Standards set forth in this Agreement. The Services shall be performed at the locations specified in Exhibit A (the "Service Locations").
1.0.2 King-Carter Corporation shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner in accordance with industry standards and best practices. King-Carter Corporation warrants that all Services shall be performed by qualified personnel with appropriate skills, training, and experience.
2. Fees and Payment
2.0.1 Bailey-David Inc. shall pay King-Carter Corporation the fees set forth in Exhibit B for the Services (the "Service Fees"). Service Fees are exclusive of all applicable taxes, which shall be the responsibility of Bailey-David Inc..
2.0.2 Service Fees shall be invoiced quarterly and are due within 30 days of the invoice date. King-Carter Corporation may suspend Services if payment is more than 15 days overdue.
3. Term and Renewal
3.0.1 This Agreement shall commence on November 14, 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue for an initial term of 29 months (the "Initial Term"). Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 24-month periods (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days prior to the end of the then-current term.
4. Warranties
4.0.1 King-Carter Corporation warrants that: (a) it has the right and authority to enter into this Agreement; (b) the Services will be performed in a professional manner consistent with industry standards; and (c) the Services will conform to the Service Standards. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS SECTION, SERVICE PROVIDER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.
5. General Provisions
5.1 Governing Law
5.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
5.2 Entire Agreement
5.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
