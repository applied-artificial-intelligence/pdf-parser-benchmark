EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: May 15, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Gordon-Dodson LLC
("Company")
and

Penny Rogers
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Gordon-Dodson LLC desires to employ Penny Rogers in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Penny Rogers wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Gordon-Dodson LLC hereby employs Penny Rogers in the position of Sales Director. Penny Rogers shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Gordon-Dodson LLC from time to time. Penny Rogers shall report to C-Suite Executive.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Gordon-Dodson LLC shall pay Penny Rogers an annual base salary of $205,000, payable in accordance with Gordon-Dodson LLC's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Penny Rogers acknowledges that during employment, Penny Rogers will have access to Confidential Information of Gordon-Dodson LLC. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Gordon-Dodson LLC's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Penny Rogers agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Gordon-Dodson LLC.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Penny Rogers hereby assigns to Gordon-Dodson LLC all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Penny Rogers, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Gordon-Dodson LLC's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Gordon-Dodson LLC; or (c) are created using Gordon-Dodson LLC's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Penny Rogers agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Gordon-Dodson LLC's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
