EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: April 26, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd.
("Company")
and

Lisa Perry
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd. desires to employ Lisa Perry in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Lisa Perry wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd. hereby employs Lisa Perry in the position of Chief Marketing Officer. Lisa Perry shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd. from time to time. Lisa Perry shall report to Board of Directors.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd. shall pay Lisa Perry an annual base salary of $280,000, payable in accordance with Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Lisa Perry acknowledges that during employment, Lisa Perry will have access to Confidential Information of Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Lisa Perry agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Lisa Perry hereby assigns to Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Lisa Perry, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd.; or (c) are created using Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Lisa Perry agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Peterson, Sullivan and Johnson Ltd.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Massachusetts, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
