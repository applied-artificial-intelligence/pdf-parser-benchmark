EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: October 10, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Acosta Enterprises
("Company")
and

William Holmes
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Acosta Enterprises desires to employ William Holmes in the specified position;
WHEREAS, William Holmes wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Acosta Enterprises hereby employs William Holmes in the position of Senior Software Engineer. William Holmes shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Acosta Enterprises from time to time. William Holmes shall report to Director.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Acosta Enterprises shall pay William Holmes an annual base salary of $125,000, payable in accordance with Acosta Enterprises's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 William Holmes acknowledges that during employment, William Holmes will have access to Confidential Information of Acosta Enterprises. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Acosta Enterprises's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. William Holmes agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Acosta Enterprises.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 William Holmes hereby assigns to Acosta Enterprises all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by William Holmes, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Acosta Enterprises's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Acosta Enterprises; or (c) are created using Acosta Enterprises's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). William Holmes agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Acosta Enterprises's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
