EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: October 10, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Brooks Group LLC
("Company")
and

Jessica Barber
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Brooks Group LLC desires to employ Jessica Barber in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Jessica Barber wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Brooks Group LLC hereby employs Jessica Barber in the position of Marketing Associate. Jessica Barber shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Brooks Group LLC from time to time. Jessica Barber shall report to Team Lead.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Brooks Group LLC shall pay Jessica Barber an annual base salary of $115,000, payable in accordance with Brooks Group LLC's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Jessica Barber acknowledges that during employment, Jessica Barber will have access to Confidential Information of Brooks Group LLC. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Brooks Group LLC's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Jessica Barber agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Brooks Group LLC.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Jessica Barber hereby assigns to Brooks Group LLC all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Jessica Barber, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Brooks Group LLC's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Brooks Group LLC; or (c) are created using Brooks Group LLC's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Jessica Barber agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Brooks Group LLC's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
