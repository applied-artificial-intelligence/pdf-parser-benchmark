CONSULTING AGREEMENT

Effective Date: August 13, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Delacruz-Stevens Ltd.
("Client")
and

Nichole Lopez
("Consultant")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Delacruz-Stevens Ltd. desires to engage Nichole Lopez to provide certain services;
WHEREAS, Nichole Lopez has expertise and is willing to provide such services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing the engagement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Services
1.0.1 Nichole Lopez agrees to provide Delacruz-Stevens Ltd. with consulting services as described in one or more Statements of Work to be mutually agreed upon by the parties (the "Services"). Each Statement of Work shall specify the scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees for the Services to be performed.
1.0.2 Nichole Lopez shall deliver to Delacruz-Stevens Ltd. the work product and deliverables described in the applicable Statement of Work ("Deliverables"). All Deliverables shall be delivered in the format and by the deadlines specified in the Statement of Work. Delacruz-Stevens Ltd. shall have 7 business days to review and approve each Deliverable.
2. Compensation
2.0.1 Delacruz-Stevens Ltd. shall pay Nichole Lopez fees as specified in the applicable Statement of Work. Unless otherwise specified, fees shall be calculated at an hourly rate of $500 per hour. Nichole Lopez shall submit monthly invoices detailing hours worked and expenses incurred, and Delacruz-Stevens Ltd. shall pay such invoices within 15 days of receipt.
2.0.2 Nichole Lopez shall be reimbursed for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with performing the Services, provided that such expenses are pre-approved by Delacruz-Stevens Ltd. in writing and are supported by appropriate documentation. Travel expenses shall be reimbursed at coach class rates for air travel and standard rates for lodging and meals.
3. Independent Contractor Relationship
3.0.1 Nichole Lopez is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or joint venturer of Delacruz-Stevens Ltd.. Nichole Lopez shall have sole control over the manner and means of performing the Services, subject to the specifications in the Statement of Work. Nichole Lopez shall be responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits relating to Nichole Lopez's performance of Services. Nichole Lopez shall not have authority to bind Delacruz-Stevens Ltd. or to make representations on behalf of Delacruz-Stevens Ltd..
4. Intellectual Property
4.0.1 All Work Product created by Nichole Lopez in connection with the Services shall be the sole and exclusive property of Delacruz-Stevens Ltd.. "Work Product" means all deliverables, documents, materials, inventions, discoveries, and other work product created or developed by Nichole Lopez in performing the Services. Nichole Lopez hereby assigns to Delacruz-Stevens Ltd. all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein.
5. General Provisions
5.1 Governing Law
5.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
5.2 Entire Agreement
5.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
