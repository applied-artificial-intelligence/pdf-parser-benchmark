EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: April 03, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Moore Solutions
("Company")
and

Andrea Luna
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Moore Solutions desires to employ Andrea Luna in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Andrea Luna wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Moore Solutions hereby employs Andrea Luna in the position of Chief Revenue Officer. Andrea Luna shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Moore Solutions from time to time. Andrea Luna shall report to President.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Moore Solutions shall pay Andrea Luna an annual base salary of $220,000, payable in accordance with Moore Solutions's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Andrea Luna acknowledges that during employment, Andrea Luna will have access to Confidential Information of Moore Solutions. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Moore Solutions's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Andrea Luna agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Moore Solutions.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Andrea Luna hereby assigns to Moore Solutions all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Andrea Luna, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Moore Solutions's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Moore Solutions; or (c) are created using Moore Solutions's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Andrea Luna agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Moore Solutions's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
