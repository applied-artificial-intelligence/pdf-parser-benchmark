EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: December 24, 2024

BY AND BETWEEN

Woods and Sons Ltd.
("Company")
and

Lawrence Nguyen
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Woods and Sons Ltd. desires to employ Lawrence Nguyen in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Lawrence Nguyen wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Woods and Sons Ltd. hereby employs Lawrence Nguyen in the position of Marketing Manager. Lawrence Nguyen shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Woods and Sons Ltd. from time to time. Lawrence Nguyen shall report to Senior Director.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Woods and Sons Ltd. shall pay Lawrence Nguyen an annual base salary of $190,000, payable in accordance with Woods and Sons Ltd.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Lawrence Nguyen acknowledges that during employment, Lawrence Nguyen will have access to Confidential Information of Woods and Sons Ltd.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Woods and Sons Ltd.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Lawrence Nguyen agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Woods and Sons Ltd..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Lawrence Nguyen hereby assigns to Woods and Sons Ltd. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Lawrence Nguyen, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Woods and Sons Ltd.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Woods and Sons Ltd.; or (c) are created using Woods and Sons Ltd.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Lawrence Nguyen agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Woods and Sons Ltd.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
