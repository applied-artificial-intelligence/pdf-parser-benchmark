CONSULTING AGREEMENT

Effective Date: August 02, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Herrera and Sons Corporation
("Client")
and

Carmen Smith
("Consultant")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Herrera and Sons Corporation desires to engage Carmen Smith to provide certain services;
WHEREAS, Carmen Smith has expertise and is willing to provide such services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing the engagement;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Services
1.0.1 Carmen Smith agrees to provide Herrera and Sons Corporation with consulting services as described in one or more Statements of Work to be mutually agreed upon by the parties (the "Services"). Each Statement of Work shall specify the scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees for the Services to be performed.
1.0.2 Carmen Smith shall deliver to Herrera and Sons Corporation the work product and deliverables described in the applicable Statement of Work ("Deliverables"). All Deliverables shall be delivered in the format and by the deadlines specified in the Statement of Work. Herrera and Sons Corporation shall have 7 business days to review and approve each Deliverable.
2. Compensation
2.0.1 Herrera and Sons Corporation shall pay Carmen Smith fees as specified in the applicable Statement of Work. Unless otherwise specified, fees shall be calculated at an hourly rate of $100 per hour. Carmen Smith shall submit monthly invoices detailing hours worked and expenses incurred, and Herrera and Sons Corporation shall pay such invoices within 45 days of receipt.
2.0.2 Carmen Smith shall be reimbursed for reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in connection with performing the Services, provided that such expenses are pre-approved by Herrera and Sons Corporation in writing and are supported by appropriate documentation. Travel expenses shall be reimbursed at coach class rates for air travel and standard rates for lodging and meals.
3. Independent Contractor Relationship
3.0.1 Carmen Smith is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or joint venturer of Herrera and Sons Corporation. Carmen Smith shall have sole control over the manner and means of performing the Services, subject to the specifications in the Statement of Work. Carmen Smith shall be responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits relating to Carmen Smith's performance of Services. Carmen Smith shall not have authority to bind Herrera and Sons Corporation or to make representations on behalf of Herrera and Sons Corporation.
4. Intellectual Property
4.0.1 All Work Product created by Carmen Smith in connection with the Services shall be the sole and exclusive property of Herrera and Sons Corporation. "Work Product" means all deliverables, documents, materials, inventions, discoveries, and other work product created or developed by Carmen Smith in performing the Services. Carmen Smith hereby assigns to Herrera and Sons Corporation all right, title, and interest in and to the Work Product, including all intellectual property rights therein.
5. General Provisions
5.1 Governing Law
5.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
5.2 Entire Agreement
5.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
