EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: October 28, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd.
("Company")
and

Brandy Carpenter
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd. desires to employ Brandy Carpenter in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Brandy Carpenter wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd. hereby employs Brandy Carpenter in the position of Chief Marketing Officer. Brandy Carpenter shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd. from time to time. Brandy Carpenter shall report to CEO.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd. shall pay Brandy Carpenter an annual base salary of $210,000, payable in accordance with Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Brandy Carpenter acknowledges that during employment, Brandy Carpenter will have access to Confidential Information of Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Brandy Carpenter agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Brandy Carpenter hereby assigns to Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Brandy Carpenter, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd.; or (c) are created using Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Brandy Carpenter agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Mccarthy, Hines and Morton Ltd.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
