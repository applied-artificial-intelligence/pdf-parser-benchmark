EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: January 20, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Lopez Systems
("Company")
and

Julia Hobbs
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Lopez Systems desires to employ Julia Hobbs in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Julia Hobbs wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Lopez Systems hereby employs Julia Hobbs in the position of Director of Marketing. Julia Hobbs shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Lopez Systems from time to time. Julia Hobbs shall report to VP.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Lopez Systems shall pay Julia Hobbs an annual base salary of $275,000, payable in accordance with Lopez Systems's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Julia Hobbs acknowledges that during employment, Julia Hobbs will have access to Confidential Information of Lopez Systems. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Lopez Systems's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Julia Hobbs agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Lopez Systems.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Julia Hobbs hereby assigns to Lopez Systems all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Julia Hobbs, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Lopez Systems's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Lopez Systems; or (c) are created using Lopez Systems's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Julia Hobbs agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Lopez Systems's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
