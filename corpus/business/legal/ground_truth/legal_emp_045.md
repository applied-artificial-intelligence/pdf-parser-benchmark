EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: April 04, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Anderson Enterprises
("Company")
and

Mrs. Mallory Browning
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Anderson Enterprises desires to employ Mrs. Mallory Browning in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Mrs. Mallory Browning wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Anderson Enterprises hereby employs Mrs. Mallory Browning in the position of Product Manager. Mrs. Mallory Browning shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Anderson Enterprises from time to time. Mrs. Mallory Browning shall report to Director.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Anderson Enterprises shall pay Mrs. Mallory Browning an annual base salary of $170,000, payable in accordance with Anderson Enterprises's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Mrs. Mallory Browning acknowledges that during employment, Mrs. Mallory Browning will have access to Confidential Information of Anderson Enterprises. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Anderson Enterprises's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Mrs. Mallory Browning agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Anderson Enterprises.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Mrs. Mallory Browning hereby assigns to Anderson Enterprises all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Mrs. Mallory Browning, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Anderson Enterprises's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Anderson Enterprises; or (c) are created using Anderson Enterprises's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Mrs. Mallory Browning agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Anderson Enterprises's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Massachusetts, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
