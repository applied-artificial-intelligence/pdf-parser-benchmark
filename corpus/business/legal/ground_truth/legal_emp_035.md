EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: June 26, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Washington Inc Inc.
("Company")
and

Jonathan Herrera
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Washington Inc Inc. desires to employ Jonathan Herrera in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Jonathan Herrera wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Washington Inc Inc. hereby employs Jonathan Herrera in the position of VP of Operations. Jonathan Herrera shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Washington Inc Inc. from time to time. Jonathan Herrera shall report to C-Suite Executive.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Washington Inc Inc. shall pay Jonathan Herrera an annual base salary of $69,000, payable in accordance with Washington Inc Inc.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Jonathan Herrera acknowledges that during employment, Jonathan Herrera will have access to Confidential Information of Washington Inc Inc.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Washington Inc Inc.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Jonathan Herrera agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Washington Inc Inc..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Jonathan Herrera hereby assigns to Washington Inc Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Jonathan Herrera, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Washington Inc Inc.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Washington Inc Inc.; or (c) are created using Washington Inc Inc.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Jonathan Herrera agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Washington Inc Inc.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
