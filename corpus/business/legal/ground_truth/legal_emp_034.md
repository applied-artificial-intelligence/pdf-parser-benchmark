EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: June 03, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc.
("Company")
and

Robin Wallace
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc. desires to employ Robin Wallace in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Robin Wallace wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc. hereby employs Robin Wallace in the position of Marketing Manager. Robin Wallace shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc. from time to time. Robin Wallace shall report to Director.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc. shall pay Robin Wallace an annual base salary of $145,000, payable in accordance with Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc.'s standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Robin Wallace acknowledges that during employment, Robin Wallace will have access to Confidential Information of Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc.. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc.'s business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Robin Wallace agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc..
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Robin Wallace hereby assigns to Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc. all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Robin Wallace, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc.'s actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc.; or (c) are created using Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc.'s equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Robin Wallace agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Wood, Martinez and Wilson Inc.'s rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
