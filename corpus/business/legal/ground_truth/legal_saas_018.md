RECITALS

WHEREAS, Chung-Marsh LLC offers a cloud-based software platform;
WHEREAS, Parsons PLC Corporation desires to subscribe to and use the Services;
WHEREAS, the parties wish to establish terms governing such use;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions
1.0.1 Chung-Marsh LLC shall provide Parsons PLC Corporation with access to the Enterprise Engine platform (the "Service"), a cloud-based software solution designed to streamline business operations and improve productivity. The Service shall be made available via the Internet in accordance with the service level agreement set forth in this Agreement.
2. Services
2.1 Service Level Agreement
2.1.1 Chung-Marsh LLC shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Service available 99.5% of the time, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance and downtime caused by factors beyond Chung-Marsh LLC's reasonable control. Scheduled maintenance shall be performed during off-peak hours with at least 72 hours' advance notice to Parsons PLC Corporation.
2.2 Support Services
2.2.1 Chung-Marsh LLC shall provide Parsons PLC Corporation with technical support services via email during normal business hours (8am-6pm PST). Response times shall be: (a) 2 hours for critical issues affecting core functionality; (b) 12 hours for high-priority issues; and (c) 48 hours for normal priority requests.
3. Subscription and Fees
3.1 Subscription Plans
3.1.1 Parsons PLC Corporation shall subscribe to the Professional plan at a monthly subscription fee of $32083. The subscription includes 100 user accounts and 50 GB of data storage. Additional users may be added at $25 per user per month.
3.2 Payment Terms
3.2.1 All fees are due and payable in advance on a monthly basis. Chung-Marsh LLC shall invoice Parsons PLC Corporation on or about the first day of each month, and payment shall be due within 15 days of the invoice date. Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.0% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.
4. Data and Privacy
4.1 Data Ownership
4.1.1 Parsons PLC Corporation retains all right, title, and interest in and to all data, content, and materials provided by Parsons PLC Corporation or its users to the Service ("Customer Data"). Chung-Marsh LLC shall not access, use, or disclose Customer Data except as necessary to provide the Service or as required by law.
4.2 Data Security
4.2.1 Chung-Marsh LLC shall implement and maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Such safeguards shall include, at a minimum: (a) data encryption in transit and at rest; (b) regular security assessments and penetration testing; (c) employee background checks and security training; and (d) incident response procedures.
5. Term and Termination
5.1 Initial Term
5.1.1 This Agreement shall commence on March 27, 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall continue for an initial term of 32 months (the "Initial Term"). Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 36-month periods (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 30 days prior to the end of the then-current term.

6.0.1 IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, INCLUDING LOST PROFITS, LOST REVENUE, OR LOST DATA, EVEN IF SUCH PARTY HAS BEEN ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES. 7.2 Entire Agreement
7.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
