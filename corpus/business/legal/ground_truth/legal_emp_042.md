EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

Effective Date: October 14, 2025

BY AND BETWEEN

Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC
("Company")
and

Jose Dixon
("Employee")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC desires to employ Jose Dixon in the specified position;
WHEREAS, Jose Dixon wishes to accept such employment;
WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms of the employment relationship;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

1. Position and Duties
1.0.1 Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC hereby employs Jose Dixon in the position of Engineering Manager. Jose Dixon shall perform such duties and responsibilities as are customarily associated with such position and such other duties as may be assigned by Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC from time to time. Jose Dixon shall report to CEO.
2. Compensation
2.1 Base Salary
2.1.1 Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC shall pay Jose Dixon an annual base salary of $275,000, payable in accordance with Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC's standard payroll practices, currently semi-monthly. This salary shall be subject to applicable withholdings and deductions.
3. At-Will Employment
3.0.1 Employee's employment with Company is at-will, meaning that either Employee or Company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. No provision of this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment contract for any specific term.
4. Confidentiality
4.0.1 Jose Dixon acknowledges that during employment, Jose Dixon will have access to Confidential Information of Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information relating to Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC's business, including trade secrets, customer lists, financial information, business plans, product specifications, and marketing strategies. Jose Dixon agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not to disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform duties for Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC.
5. Intellectual Property Assignment
5.0.1 Jose Dixon hereby assigns to Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC all right, title, and interest in and to any inventions, discoveries, works of authorship, and other intellectual property created by Jose Dixon, alone or with others, during the term of employment that: (a) relate to Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC's actual or anticipated business; (b) result from work performed for Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC; or (c) are created using Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC's equipment, supplies, facilities, or Confidential Information (collectively, "Work Product"). Jose Dixon agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect Wright, Serrano and Holmes LLC's rights in Work Product.
6. General Provisions
6.1 Governing Law
6.1.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of law principles.
6.2 Entire Agreement
6.2.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.
