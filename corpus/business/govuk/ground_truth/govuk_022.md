## Details

This has been published to support the [end of year epidemiology reports in the low risk area (LRA)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/bovine-tb-epidemiology-reports-2024) . It explains where the data comes from, methodology used, definitions and control protocols for TB .

The text is taken from the explanatory text published in reports from previous years, updated for the current reporting year and is published as a separate reference document to reduce the length of the data and epidemiology reports.
