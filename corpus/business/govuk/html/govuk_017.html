Details

This statistical release contains data on education and training in the UK. It provides an integrated overview using data collected from:

  • England
  • Wales
  • Northern Ireland
  • Scotland

It has data on education including:

  • pupils and teachers
  • attendance
  • information on further education and higher education institutions, staff and students
  • qualifications gained
  • education expenditure
