Market Research Summary
Consumer Behavior Study - November 2025
# Methodology
This study employed multiple research methods:
- Online surveys (n=1,200)
- Focus groups (8 sessions, 6-8 participants each)
- In-depth interviews (15 subjects)

Demographic Preferences

| 35-54 | 45% | 40% | 15% |
| 55+ | 25% | 60% | 15% |
