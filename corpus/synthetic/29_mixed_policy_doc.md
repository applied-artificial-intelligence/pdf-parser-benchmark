Employee Handbook: Section 5
# 5.1 Time Off Policy
Employees are entitled to the following time off benefits:
- Vacation Time
  - 0-2 years: 15 days/year
  - 3-5 years: 20 days/year
  - 6+ years: 25 days/year
- Sick Leave
  - Unlimited sick days
  - Doctor's note required after 3 consecutive days
