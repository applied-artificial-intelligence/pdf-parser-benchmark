# Key Findings
Our research identified three critical areas for improvement:
- Customer Experience
The current checkout process has a 35% abandonment rate. Streamlining the flow could reduce this significantly.
- System Performance
Database queries are the primary bottleneck. Implementing caching would improve response times by an estimated 60%.
- Mobile Optimization
Mobile traffic represents 70% of visitors but only 40% of conversions. The interface needs responsive design improvements.
These findings form the basis for our Q3 roadmap priorities.
